# Cadence

Cadence is an online learning platform where learners buy and take courses.

## Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask to get their money back for a course they bought. It is either requested, approved or declined.
_Avoid_: Cancel my purchase, cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Refund**:
The money sent back to the learner once a Refund Request is approved. It is always the full amount the learner paid, never the list price.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, partial refund

**Refund Window**:
The rules a purchase must meet for a Refund Request: within 14 calendar days of purchase, under 2 hours of video watched, and under 50% of lessons completed. Only the buyer can make one; a claimed team seat never qualifies.
_Avoid_: Cancel window

**Chargeback**:
Not used in Cadence. It means a bank pulling money back after a card dispute, which is a different thing from a Refund.
